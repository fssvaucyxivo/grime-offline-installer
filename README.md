## About

GRIME is a fast-paced action RPG where you play as a living black hole that can absorb enemies and use their powers against them. Developed by Clover Bite, it features a dark surreal world with challenging combat, metroidvania exploration, and a deep lore built around decaying stone beings. The game blends soulslike difficulty with unique absorption mechanics.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **High Difficulty** — Souls-like combat demands patience and precise timing
- **No Official Multiplayer** — Single-player only experience
- **Platform Availability** — Primarily PC; console ports limited
- **DLC Fragmentation** — Additional content sold separately
